# Part 2 · Chapter 11 — DRAFT

**CHAPTER 11**
## The Burning Bush

Moses grew up in Pharaoh's palace. But he never forgot that he was a Hebrew.

One day, Moses saw an Egyptian beating a Hebrew worker. Moses rushed in to stop him, but he acted in anger, and the Egyptian lost his life. When Pharaoh heard what had happened, he was furious, so Moses ran away to the land of **Midian**.

Joesiah's eyes went wide. "Moses got in that much trouble?"

"He did," Grandma said gently. "Moses wanted to protect his people, but he acted in anger instead of waiting on God. The Bible tells the truth about its heroes, even their mistakes. And God still had a plan for Moses."

In Midian, Moses sat down by a well. When some shepherds chased away seven sisters who had come to water their flock, Moses stood up for them and watered their sheep himself.

"A well again!" Malani said.

"Just like Rebekah," Grandma smiled. Moses married one of the sisters, **Zipporah**, the daughter of a priest named **Jethro**, and became a shepherd.

Meanwhile, back in Egypt, the Israelites cried out under their slavery.

> "God heard their groaning and he remembered his covenant with Abraham, with Isaac and with Jacob." —Exodus 2:24

Joesiah sat up straight. "Abraham, Isaac and Jacob! That's the whole book!"

"That's the whole book," Grandma said. "God never forgot His promise."

One day, while Moses was watching the sheep near **Horeb**, the mountain of God, he saw something strange: a bush on fire that did not burn up. When Moses came closer, God called to him from the bush: "Take off your sandals, for the place where you are standing is holy ground."

God said He had seen the misery of His people and heard them crying out. He was sending Moses to bring them out of Egypt.

But Moses was afraid. "Who am I?" he asked. He said he wasn't a good speaker.

When Moses asked God's name, God answered:

> "I AM WHO I AM." —Exodus 3:14

God promised to be with Moses and sent his brother **Aaron** to speak alongside him.

Joesiah thought about it. "Moses didn't feel ready."

"Most people God calls don't feel ready," Grandma said. "God doesn't need you to be perfect. He needs you to say yes."

**ON TODAY'S MAP**
- **Midian:** in northwestern **Saudi Arabia** today, along the Gulf of Aqaba.
- **Horeb (Mount Sinai):** its exact location is debated. A traditional site is **Jebel Musa ("Mountain of Moses") in Egypt's Sinai Peninsula**.

**READ IT FOR YOURSELF:** Exodus 2:11–25, Exodus 3 and Exodus 4:10–17

### Notes for you (not part of the book)
- **Length:** about 460 words.
- **Scripture:** Exodus 2:24 and 3:14 are quoted from the NIV. "Take off your sandals…" is Exodus 3:5 (NIV). "Who am I?" is from Exodus 3:11, and Moses' worry about speaking from Exodus 4:10. The rest follows Exodus 2:11–22, 3:1–10 and 4:14–16.
- **Why Moses ran (softened at your request):** "he acted in anger, and the Egyptian lost his life." That's still true to Exodus 2:11–15, but less graphic. Grandma says Moses wanted to protect his people but didn't wait on God, and that God still had a plan for him.
- **Midian's location:** most scholars place it in northwest Arabia. Some include parts of the Sinai.
- **Illustration idea:** Moses barefoot before the burning bush, with his sheep nearby. Grandma, Joesiah and Malani are also barefoot, shoes in hand.
