# Book 2 · Chapter 14 — DRAFT

**CHAPTER 14**
## Freedom With a Purpose

Three months after leaving Egypt, the Israelites camped at the foot of **Mount Sinai**. There, God spoke to them:

> "You yourselves have seen what I did to Egypt, and how I carried you on eagles' wings and brought you to myself." —Exodus 19:4

Then God gave them the **Ten Commandments**. And listen to how they begin:

> "I am the LORD your God, who brought you out of Egypt, out of the land of slavery. You shall have no other gods before me." —Exodus 20:2–3

Joesiah thought about it. "God reminded them He set them free *before* He told them the rules."

"Exactly," Grandma said. "God's commandments weren't new chains. They were a way to live free: to love God, and to love and respect each other."

Then Grandma turned to one more verse.

> "Miriam and Aaron began to talk against Moses because of his Cushite wife, for he had married a Cushite." —Numbers 12:1

Joesiah's head popped up. "Cush! We learned about Cush in the first book!"

"We did," Grandma smiled. "Cush was connected with the lands south of Egypt: Nubia and Kush. Some Bible readers believe this was Zipporah. Others believe Moses married again. But when Miriam and Aaron spoke against Moses, God was not pleased with them."

Grandma unrolled the family tree one last time. "Look how far we've come."

ABRAHAM — A covenant begins
ISAAC — The promise continues
JACOB — A family grows
EGYPT AND ISRAEL — A nation is formed
MOSES — A people are freed

"God made a promise to one man who had no children," Grandma said, "and generations later, a whole nation walked out of slavery into freedom."

Malani held up her fingers. "Promise… kept!"

> Know the Word. Seek the Truth. Remember who you are. Walk in the purpose God gave you.

Joesiah looked at Grandma. "But you said God's promise to Abraham was for *all peoples on earth*. When does that part happen?"

Grandma's eyes sparkled. "That, my love, is where we're going next."
