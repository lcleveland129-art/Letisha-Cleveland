# Book 2 · Chapter 1 — DRAFT for author editing

**CHAPTER 1**
## Back to the Promise

Joesiah couldn't sit still. "Grandma, are we going to meet Moses now?"

Grandma smiled and unrolled the old family-tree scroll across the table. "Soon. But to understand how God set His people free, we have to go back to a promise He made long before Moses was born."

Malani climbed onto Grandma's lap. "A promise!"

Grandma's finger traced the names. "Remember Noah's son Shem? Generations passed through his family line. Then we come to a man named Terah."

She pointed to three names under Terah: Abram, Nahor and Haran.

"Abram," Joesiah read slowly. "Is that Abraham?"

"It will be," Grandma said. "But not yet."

The Bible tells us that Terah's family lived in **Ur of the Chaldeans**. Terah took his son Abram, Abram's wife **Sarai**, and his grandson **Lot**, and they set out for the land of Canaan. But when they reached a place called **Haran**, they settled there.

Scripture also tells us something important about Abram and Sarai:

> "Now Sarai was childless because she was not able to conceive." —Genesis 11:30

Joesiah frowned. "But Grandma, if they didn't have any children, how could their family become a whole nation?"

Grandma's eyes sparkled. "That is exactly the right question. Hold on to it. The answer is the whole story of this book."

**ON TODAY'S MAP**
- **Ur:** an ancient city in what is now **southern Iraq**. Most scholars connect it with "Ur of the Chaldeans." Some suggest other places.
- **Haran:** near the town of **Harran in southeastern Turkey**.

Malani pointed at the long road on the map between Ur and Haran. "Far!"

"Very far," Grandma said. "And Abram's journey was only beginning. Sometimes God's promises start in the places where it looks impossible."
