## 1. What caused the Chapter 2 / Chapter 7 error on pages 6–7

The searchable text was in the right order. The problem was in the **pictures**, and there were three separate causes:

1. **The chapter numbers are printed inside the artwork, and they came from an older 17-chapter draft.** The picture on page 7 was the one printed "**CHAPTER 7 – Ham's Descendants and the Lands of Africa**". Its subject is today's **Chapter 4** (Ham's family). Nine of the 13 pictures had out-of-date labels printed in them: "CHAPTER" 7, 8, 9, 10, 11, 12, 15 and 17 (eight pictures), plus "PART 2" on the preview. The Chapter 13 picture also says "Key Lessons from Part 1".
2. **Every picture sat on the wrong side of the page turn.** Each picture was on a right-hand page *before* its chapter text. In a printed book, the open pages always showed chapter *N*'s text beside chapter *N + 1*'s picture. That's why Chapter 2 appeared beside the next picture in line.
3. **Two pictures were swapped.** "Ham's Descendants" (Chapter 4's subject) and "Noah's Sons and Their Families" (Chapter 3's subject) were in each other's places.

The pictures also had old page numbers printed in them (1, 4, 14, 15, 16, 15, 17 …) that did not match the real page numbers.

## 2. How it was fixed

- **Each chapter is now a facing pair of pages:** chapter text on the left-hand page, its own picture on the right-hand page. You see both together when the book is open. The exception is Chapter 10, which faces a Scripture Spotlight page because its picture now illustrates Chapter 12.
- **Each picture was matched to its chapter by its subject**, not by the number printed on it.
- **The out-of-date chapter numbers inside the pictures were relettered** (for example 7 → 4, 8 → 6, 15 → 11, 17 → 13). Only the lettering was touched. The new lettering matches the size, position and colour of the original.
- **The old page numbers inside the pictures were replaced** with the true page numbers.
- **The Book 2 preview now appears once, on the last page**, after Chapter 14.

## 5. Artwork corrections (approved artwork preserved)

- **Kept as-is:**
  - The characters (Grandma, Joesiah and Malani), their faces, hairstyles and skin tones, and every scene.
  - No picture was regenerated, repainted, stretched or AI-enhanced.
- **Cropped:**
  - Only the photographed-book mock-up frame was removed: about 34 px on each side, 12 px at the top and 36 px at the bottom of the 980 × 1268 originals.
  - No characters or lettering were cut off.
- **Enlarged:**
  - Lanczos resampling ×2.5 (a standard high-quality, non-AI method) to 2280 × 3050 px, with very light sharpening.
  - This gives 302 DPI at the printed size of 7.55 × 10.1 in.
  - See the resolution warning in Open Issues #7: enlarging cannot add detail that wasn't in the originals.
- **Relettered:**
  - 9 chapter/series labels, 13 page numbers and 1 Scripture misprint.
  - Chapter 4 Canaan panel: "(Levant region)" → "(today: Israel, Palestine, Lebanon, and parts of Syria and Jordan)". This also cleaned up garbled strike-through marks.
  - Chapter 6 paragraph: "After the flood, Ham's descendants built great nations in Africa" → "After the flood, the families of Ham's sons Cush, Mizraim, and Put built great nations in Africa".
  - Chapter 9 quotation box: a summary sentence wrongly cited as "Genesis 37:28, 36" → Joseph's actual words, "You intended to harm me, but God intended it for good." (Genesis 50:20).
  - The Chapter 12 "A Faithful Servant" panel: "Pharaoh's house" → "Potiphar's house" (Genesis 39:1–4). All three lines were relettered together.
  - The first paragraph of the Chapter 5 "What Happened with Canaan?" box. It wrongly said "Noah saw something wrong and responded in anger". It now reads: "In Genesis 9, Ham saw his father uncovered and told his brothers. Because of Ham's actions, Noah cursed Ham's son, Canaan:"
  - The old lettering was removed with inpainting (filling in from the surrounding background), and new lettering set in a matching small-caps serif.
  - Size, position and colour were sampled from the original label.
- **Placement:**
  - Pictures sit inside a thin gold rule on a deep-navy full-bleed background.
  - All lettering inside the pictures (titles, captions, quote boxes) stays at least 0.45 in inside the trim.
  - This keeps it inside KDP's safe area. Full-bleed placement would have put the art's top titles into the trim zone.
