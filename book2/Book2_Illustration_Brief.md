# Illustration Brief: *Joesiah & Malani: From Abraham to Moses — The Journey to Freedom*

**Series:** Joesiah & Malani, Book 2 · **Author:** LeTisha C. Cleveland · **Readers:** ages 8–12
**What's needed:** 15 interior illustrations + 1 front cover + 1 back cover (17 pieces in total)

---

## 1. The 5 most important rules (please read first)

1. **NO TEXT IN THE ARTWORK.** No chapter numbers, page numbers, titles, captions, Bible verses or labels. The designer adds all text during layout.
   - *Why:* in Book 1, chapter numbers painted into the art ended up on the wrong pages, and every one had to be repaired by hand.
   - **Leave clear, calm space** (sky, wall, sand) in the **top 20%** of each picture so a title can sit there.
2. **Deliver the flat artwork only.** No "photo of a book" mock-ups, page curls, frames, borders, shadows or backgrounds around the picture.
3. **High resolution:**
   - **Interior pictures:** at least **2,550 × 3,400 pixels**, portrait, 3:4 ratio.
   - **Covers:** the sizes in section 5.
   - Book 1's art was only about 1,000 pixels wide and printed soft. Please don't upscale small images to reach these sizes; create or export at full size.
4. **Keep the main characters identical to Book 1** (section 2). Same faces, skin tones, hair and clothing on every page.
5. **Safe area:** keep faces, hands and any important detail at least **0.5 inch (150 px)** inside every edge. Edges may be trimmed when the book is printed.

**File format:** PNG or maximum-quality JPG, RGB colour, 300 DPI.
**File names:** `B2_Ch01.png` … `B2_Ch14.png`, `B2_Book3_Preview.png`, `B2_Cover_Front.png`, `B2_Cover_Back.png`.

Reference pictures are in the `illustration_reference` folder:

| Character | Appearance (keep the same in every picture) |

## 3. Overall style and cultural guidance

**Style:**
- Match Book 1: vibrant, warm, painterly, semi-realistic digital art.
- Golden-hour and sunrise light, rich colour, cinematic depth.
- Gold and deep-navy accents, which are the series colours.

**People:**
- Show the people of the ancient Near East, Egypt and Nubia **authentically**, with a natural range of **brown skin tones**.
- Hebrews/Israelites, Egyptians, Midianites and Cushites/Nubians should look like the peoples of that region. **Please avoid European-default depictions.**

**Clothing and settings:** use ancient styles and places, not modern ones:
- Tents and caravans for Abraham's family.
- Egyptian linen, collars, columns and hieroglyph-carved walls.
- Papyrus, the Nile, desert mountains.

**God:**
- **Do not show God's face or body.**
- Show God's presence as **light, fire, cloud or glow**: the burning bush, the pillars of cloud and fire, light from heaven.
- Angels may appear as glowing figures of light.

**Keep it gentle for ages 8–12:**
- No blood, weapons in use, injuries, death or frightening violence. The scene notes below give the specific limits for each chapter.

**Repeating motif:** Grandma's **family-tree scroll** appears in Chapters 1, 8 and 14. Please keep it the same design: an old parchment scroll with a gold-leaf tree.

## 4. Scene list — one picture per chapter

*"Our three" = Grandma, Joesiah and Malani.*

### Ch 1 — Back to the Promise
- **Scene:** our three at a table with the family-tree scroll unrolled. A glowing path on the scroll runs from Noah's son Shem down to the name "Abram". Show it as a glowing line with small leaf shapes; **no readable writing**.
- **Background:** a faint, dreamlike vision of an ancient city (Ur) with a stepped temple tower (ziggurat), and a road heading toward distant hills (Haran).
- **Mood:** wonder, the start of a journey.

### Ch 2 — Abram Answers the Call
- **Scene:** sunrise. Abram, about 75, with a grey beard and Near Eastern robes, sets out with his wife Sarai, his nephew Lot, servants, donkeys, camels, sheep and goats.
- **Our three:** walking alongside the caravan. Malani rides on Grandma's hip or holds her hand.
- **Mood:** brave faith, stepping into the unknown.

### Ch 3 — A Sky Full of Stars
- **Scene:** night in the desert. Abram stands outside his tent, gazing up at a sky **blazing with countless stars**, with the Milky Way overhead.
- **Our three:** nearby. Malani counts on her fingers, overwhelmed and delighted.
- **Mood:** awe. The promise is bigger than anyone can count.

### Ch 4 — Hagar: The God Who Sees
- **Scene:** Hagar, a young **Egyptian** woman in simple servant's clothing, kneels by a spring in the wilderness. A soft heavenly light (an angel as a glowing figure) shines down on her.
- **Our three:** a respectful distance away.
- **Mood:** comfort; she is seen and loved. Gentle, not sad.

### Ch 5 — Isaac, the Son of Laughter
- **Scene:** on a mountaintop at dawn, Abraham and young Isaac (about 10–12) look up in relief at a **ram caught by its horns in a thicket**. A bundle of firewood lies nearby.
- **NOT allowed:** a knife, altar, ropes or binding.
- **Alternative if you prefer:** elderly Abraham and Sarah laughing with joy as they hold baby Isaac.
- **Mood:** relief and gratitude. "God provides."

### Ch 6 — Rebekah and the Twins
- **Scene:** at a stone well outside a town, Rebekah, a young woman with a water jar on her shoulder, pours water into a trough for **ten thirsty camels**. Abraham's servant, an older man, watches in amazement.
- **Our three:** Malani "helps" with a tiny jar.
- **Mood:** kindness, warmth, a little humour.

### Ch 7 — A Ladder to Heaven and a New Name
- **Scene:** night. Jacob, a young man, sleeps on the ground with a stone for a pillow. Above him a **glowing stairway** rises into the starry sky, with angels of light going up and down it.
- **Our three:** looking up in wonder.
- **Mood:** grace, God's presence.

### Ch 8 — Twelve Sons, Twelve Tribes
- **Scene:** an Egyptian-style room. Old Jacob (Israel) sits up in bed and **crosses his arms** to bless two boys: his right hand on the younger, Ephraim, his left on the older, Manasseh.
- **Also in the scene:** Joseph in Egyptian official dress and his Egyptian wife **Asenath**.
- **Our three:** the family-tree scroll unrolled beside them.
- **Mood:** family, blessing, many peoples joined together.

### Ch 9 — A New King Who Did Not Know Joseph
- **Scene:** a grand Egyptian throne room. Two midwives, **Shiphrah and Puah**, stand calm and courageous before a stern Pharaoh on his throne.
- **Background:** through a window, Israelite workers make mud bricks under the sun. Show hardship, but no whips in use.
- **Our three:** watch from behind a column.
- **Mood:** quiet courage.

### Ch 10 — A Baby in a Basket
- **Scene:** the Nile at morning among tall papyrus. An **Egyptian princess** (Pharaoh's daughter) and her attendants lift a reed basket from the water; baby Moses is inside.
- **Miriam:** a girl of about 10–12, peeks from the reeds nearby.
- **Our three:** hidden in the reeds beside Miriam. Malani covers a delighted gasp.
- **Mood:** tenderness, hope.

### Ch 11 — The Burning Bush
- **Scene:** a rocky mountainside. Moses, now an adult shepherd with a staff and his sandals off, kneels before a **bush filled with bright fire that isn't burning up**. His sheep graze nearby.
- **Our three:** also barefoot, shoes in hand.
- **Mood:** holy awe.

### Ch 12 — "Let My People Go"
- **Scene:** night, inside a simple Israelite home lit by oil lamps. A family shares the Passover meal: roasted lamb, flat bread, bitter herbs. They're dressed for travel, with sandals on, staffs in hand and bags packed by the door.
- **The doorframe:** show the doorway from inside, with a lamb-marked doorframe suggested **subtly**. A dark mark is fine; no dripping blood.
- **Our three:** at the table with the family.
- **Mood:** protection, family, anticipation.

### Ch 13 — A Path Through the Sea
- **Scene:** on the far shore at dawn, **Miriam** (now an adult woman) dances joyfully with a tambourine, and other women dance with her.
- **Background:** behind them, towering walls of water and the crowd of Israelites, with flocks, still crossing on dry ground. The **pillar of fire/cloud** glows above.
- **Our three:** Malani twirls beside Miriam.
- **NOT allowed:** soldiers or chariots in the water.
- **Mood:** victory, celebration, freedom. This is the most joyful picture in the book.

### Ch 14 — Freedom With a Purpose
- **Scene:** Mount Sinai at dawn, wrapped in glowing cloud. Moses comes down holding **two stone tablets**. Show them blank or with faint marks; no readable text.
- **Below:** the Israelite camp with tents.
- **Our three:** sit on a rock with the family-tree scroll, looking out over everything they've seen.
- **Mood:** peace, purpose, a journey completed.

### Book 3 Preview page (last page of the book)
- **Scene:** our three on a hill at sunset, looking down a long winding road that leads from Mount Sinai in the distance toward a small town, **Bethlehem**, under one **bright star**.
- **Title space:** leave space in the top third for the title *"From Moses to Yeshua: The Promise Fulfilled"*, which the designer adds.
- **Mood:** hope, "the journey continues."
- **Please don't show** Yeshua (Jesus) himself on this page. The author will decide how he appears in Book 3.

## 5. Covers

The book will be printed by Amazon KDP: 8.5 × 11 inch paperback, glossy, with 0.125 inch bleed.

### Front cover: the Red Sea crossing
- **Scene:** the Israelites walk on dry ground between two towering walls of water. Moses stands on a rock at the front with his staff raised. The pillar of fire glows over the water.
- **Our three:** in the foreground, walking toward the viewer: Grandma in the middle, holding Joesiah's and Malani's hands. Make them large enough to recognize; they are the series' "face".
- **Text space:** leave clear space in the **top 30%** for the title and the bottom **12%** for the author banner. **No text in the art.**
- **Size:** **2,625 × 3,375 pixels** (8.75 × 11.25 inches at 300 DPI, which includes the bleed).

### Back cover
- **Scene:** a calm continuation of the same world: the far shore at sunrise, the sea calm again, the desert path ahead.
- **Text space:** keep the upper two-thirds soft and simple so the description text can sit on it.
- **Barcode:** leave the **bottom-right 2.5 × 1.75 inch area** plain. Amazon places the barcode there.
- **Size:** **2,625 × 3,375 pixels.**

The designer assembles the spine and full wrap, so you don't need to provide them.
